CounterRing Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

CounterRing is published by VS Gamerz. This policy covers the Android app CounterRing (com.vsgamerz.counterring).

Data stored on your device

Level progress and settings stay on your device. CounterRing does not require an account and works offline for play.

Ads

The app may show ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect device and advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads, including through the Google consent form where required. See Google’s privacy policy.

Analytics and crashes

The app uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand usage and fix crashes. This can include app events, device type, and crash logs. See Firebase privacy information.

What we do not do

We do not sell your play data. We do not ask you to sign in. There are no in-app purchases in the current version.

One-Pan Rush Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game One-Pan Rush (Android package com.vsgamerz.onepanrush) handles information. One-Pan Rush is a free cooking game made by VS Gamerz, an individual developer in India. It is not made by a company. In this policy, "we" and "the developer" mean VS Gamerz.

Summary

  • We do not ask you for a name, email address, phone number or an account. There is no sign-in.
  • Your progress and settings are saved only on your device.
  • The game uses Google AdMob to show ads (a full-screen ad after some wins, and an optional rewarded ad you can choose to watch). Where the law requires it, Google's consent form is shown before any ad is requested.
  • The game includes Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics so we can understand how the game is played and fix crashes. Those tools stay off until a Firebase configuration file is present in the build and, where consent is required, until you allow analytics storage.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

Information stored on your device

The game keeps a small save file on your device. It contains:

  • which services you have unlocked, and your best stars, best score, attempts and wins for each service;
  • whether you have finished the tutorial and which intro cards you have already seen;
  • your settings (sound effects, haptics, left-hand layout);
  • local ad-pacing counters (wins since the last full-screen ad, and timestamps used to space ads out);
  • a session counter, and a record of whether ads may be requested after the last consent result.

This file stays on your device. It is not included in Android cloud backups. A mid-service attempt is not saved: if the app is closed during a service, that service starts again. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in your device settings, deletes this file.

Ad consent choices are stored by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), not in our save file (we only keep a local flag of whether ads may be requested).

Information collected by third-party services

Google AdMob and consent

One-Pan Rush uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) only. There is no ad mediation and no other ad network. The game may show:

  • a full-screen interstitial ad after some won services, when you tap Continue, from Service 4 onward (not during play, not in the tutorial, and not in Services 1–3);
  • an optional rewarded ad on a lost service from Service 4, labelled Calm retry, which you choose to watch. Retry without an ad is always available.

There are no banner ads during play, and no ads that open when you launch the app.

To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (which can be used to estimate your general location), ad interactions, and device and app information. Google acts as an independent controller of that advertising data.

Consent: where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), Google's UMP consent form is shown after the splash screen and before any ad is requested. Ads are not loaded until that step allows them. You can choose personalised ads, non-personalised ads, or to refuse ads, according to the options Google shows in the form.

To change your choice later, open the game's Settings and, if it is shown, tap Privacy choices. That row appears only when Google's UMP says a privacy-options form is required; it then opens Google's form again. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads), or clear the game's storage and go through the consent form again.

How Google uses this information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics (Google)

The game includes Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase) and Firebase Crashlytics. Google processes this data for us as a service provider.

Analytics may include: custom events about play (for example starting or finishing a service, tutorial steps, settings changes, and whether an ad was shown or skipped), automatic events such as first open and session start, device and app information (device model, OS version, app version, language, and a country or region estimated from IP address), an app-instance identifier, and, when present, the advertising ID. Event parameters do not include your name, email address, or a device identifier we typed in ourselves. We do not set a user ID.

Crashlytics may include crash logs, device and app information, and short diagnostic keys such as the current service, screen name, tutorial step and ad state. It can also record non-fatal errors (for example a failed save or a failed ad load, with ad unit IDs removed from the message).

Collection defaults to off. Analytics collection is turned on only after consent is obtained or is not required, and only when analytics storage is granted. If a Firebase configuration file is not present in the build, Analytics and Crashlytics stay uninitialised and do not send data.

Firebase privacy information: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

What we collect ourselves

We do not run our own servers for this game, and we do not collect personal data from you directly in the app. If you email us, we will see the address and the content of your message so we can reply.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads and, when it is initialised, to send analytics and crash reports. Play itself does not need a network connection.
  • Advertising ID: used by AdMob for advertising, and by Firebase Analytics for analytics when collection is on.
  • Wake lock: added by the ads libraries, not used by our own code to run a background service.

The game does not use your contacts, precise location, microphone, camera, or photos.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Change ad consent as described above, when the Privacy choices row is available, or through your device's ad settings.
  • Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage, deletes the local save file.
  • To ask us to help with a deletion request for data held by Google on our behalf (Analytics or Crashlytics), email psgameplay121@gmail.com. We will act on requests within 30 days. We do not hold a player account that we can look up by name, so we may need details you can share about the device or the approximate time you played.

Children

One-Pan Rush is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ad requests are not tagged as child-directed. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data sent to Google's services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Changes to this policy

We will update this page before releasing any version that changes how we handle your information, and we will change the effective date above.
